Give the LIGHT functions your own names

The display names are edited on the Beoremote One. For the Horizon, the editor is in a separate infrared product profile:

  1. On the remote, open LIST → Settings → Advanced → Products. Show or add the separate Beovision (infrared/IR) profile if it isn't listed yet. On remotes with a Show menu, enable it there.
  2. Select that separate Beovision product from the product list at the top of LIST.
  3. Open Settings → Advanced → Light menu. Use Rename for names, Show for visible functions and Move for their order.
  4. Switch back to your usual Bluetooth-paired Horizon product. The renamed LIGHT functions remain available there.

Keep your Horizon pairing. The extra IR profile only gives you access to this editor. A name such as “Movie” is a remote label; choose the matching HA scene separately in your automation.
